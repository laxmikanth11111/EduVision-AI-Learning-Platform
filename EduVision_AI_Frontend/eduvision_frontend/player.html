The water cycle

The sun heats water in oceans and lakes, causing it to evaporate into vapor. The vapor rises, cools, and condenses into clouds. Once the droplets are heavy enough, they fall as rain — then flow back to the ocean, and the cycle repeats.

Photosynthesis

Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil. Using sunlight captured by chlorophyll, they convert these into glucose for energy, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.
